Why Festival Dates Depend on Your Location

Vedic festival determinations (such as Ekadashi or Janmashtami midnight Nishita Kala) are calculated from the local sunrise (*Udaya Tithi*) and local celestial midnight at your specific longitude. Copying an Indian calendar in Phoenix or New York can lead to celebrating on the wrong day. Bhakti Companion calculates each observance according to your chosen city's astronomical times.
