# Product brief

## Purpose

Bhakti Companion helps Hindu families understand the day’s observance, find devotional songs and prayers in accessible scripts, and prepare a respectful home program.

## First-release promise

- Location-aware curated calendar cards for supported locations.
- Hindi, Bengali, Sanskrit, Roman transliteration, and English meaning where reviewed.
- On-device favorites and Home Program preparation.
- No accounts, ads, analytics, server-side inference, or background tracking.

## Explicit limits

The app does not yet calculate a universal panchang, determine fasting dates for every tradition, host licensed recordings, or provide offline support. It must not imply those capabilities.

## Acceptance criteria for new features

A feature must identify its user problem, content/rights boundary, local-data or network behavior, mobile and keyboard behavior, failure state, regression coverage, and reviewer/rollback plan.
