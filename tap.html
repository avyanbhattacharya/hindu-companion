Tap Japa Counter & Digital Mala

The Tap Japa Counter acts as a digital mala for manual repetition. Each press of the large TAP +1 button updates your total count, completed 108-naam rounds, and active progress.

Your count is saved in local browser storage (`localStorage`), allowing you to pause, close the tab, or reload without losing progress. For hands-free practice, switch to Touchless Japa Counter.
