## A family-centered observance

Janmashtami celebrates the appearance of Sri Krishna. Families may decorate a home altar, sing kirtan, read Krishna's birth pastimes, and share prasadam according to their own tradition.

## Simple preparation

- Confirm local date and any fasting times with a trusted panchang.
- Prepare flowers, a small lamp, and a few songs everyone can join.
- Invite children to make gentle footprints or draw a small Vrindavan scene.

The spirit is remembrance and loving service—not performance.
