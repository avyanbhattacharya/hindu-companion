## What it is

Ekadashi occurs twice in a lunar month. Many Hindu traditions observe it with additional prayer, japa, study, and a grain-free or otherwise adjusted diet.

## At home

- Check a trusted local panchang for the date and parana window.
- Choose an observance appropriate for age, health, and family practice.
- Keep the day simple: prayer, reading, kirtan, and kindness are often more meaningful than complexity.

## Important note

Fasting practices differ among sampradayas and households. This page is a learning guide, not medical advice or an authoritative calendar.
