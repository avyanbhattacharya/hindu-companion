## Text

गोविन्द जय जय गोपाल जय जय ।
राधा रमण हरि गोविन्द जय जय ॥

*Govinda jaya jaya gopala jaya jaya / Radha-ramana hari govinda jaya jaya*

## Meaning (Draft)

Glory to Govinda, Glory to Gopala, the lord Hari who brings joy to Radha.
