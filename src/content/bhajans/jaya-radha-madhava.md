## Text

जय राधा माधव कुञ्ज बिहारी ।  
गोपी जन वल्लभ गिरिवर धारी ॥

जशोदा नन्दन ब्रज जन रंजन ।  
यमुना तीर वन चारी ॥

*Jaya Radha-Madhava, Kunja-bihari / Gopi-jana-vallabha, Giri-vara-dhari.*

## Meaning

Glories to Radha and Madhava, who enjoy loving pastimes in the forest groves; beloved of the gopis and lifter of Govardhana Hill.
